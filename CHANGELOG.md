# Changelog

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-06)
- Tents, awnings, laundry and other hanging cloth: no more dark wedges and lines on the cloth where the sun shines through it (for example inside a tent), and no dark line where a tent wall meets the floor. The sunlit side keeps its contact shadows.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-05)
- Fixed a dark square around the sun when facing a low sun (sunrise, sunset), mostly at low resolutions such as the Steam Deck's, and dark bands on flat ground with a low sun from the side.

## 1.0 (2026-11-04)
- First release.
